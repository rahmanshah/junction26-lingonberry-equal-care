## Where we lag

- In Kymenlaakson hyvinvointialue, the crude curative treatment share (not adjusted for age or stage) was 19.3% (95% CI 16.3–22.7; n=114/591) compared with the 2019–2023 national value of 27.0% (4009/14873), and the 95% confidence interval excludes the national value. `cty:curative:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, bs:coverage:Diagnosis`
- In values digitised from Kuva 16 with a precision of ±0.5 percentage points, this curative treatment gap holds after age adjustment for men at 17.9% (95% CI 13.9–22.9) versus the national reference line of 26.7%, where the 95% confidence interval excludes the national value, whereas for women the age-adjusted share is 22.6% (95% CI 16.8–29.3) versus 26.9% nationally, where the 95% confidence interval includes the national value. `k16:curative:men:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:women:national`
- Conversely, crude shares for non-curative care exceeded national benchmarks, with life-prolonging treatment at 37.6% (95% CI 33.8–41.5; n=222/591) versus 30.4% (4522/14873) nationally and symptomatic treatment at 51.3% (95% CI 47.2–55.3; n=303/591) versus 39.5% (5871/14873) nationally; each 95% confidence interval excludes the national value, and these treatment categories overlap because a patient can receive several treatment types within 12 months. `cty:life_prolonging:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Multidisciplinary team (MDT) meetings were recorded for 0.5% (95% CI 0.2–1.5; n=3/591) of patients in Kymenlaakson hyvinvointialue compared with 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value, but because HILMO captures specialised care procedures only from direct patient contacts, this indicator measures recording practice rather than care. `cty:mdt_recorded:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`

## How sure are we

- The crude share of patients with no treatment record within 12 months in Kymenlaakson hyvinvointialue is 16.4% (95% CI 13.7–19.6; n=97/591) compared with 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value; because this category mixes untreated patients and unrecorded treatment, a share below the national benchmark indicates relatively complete treatment recording, meaning the lower curative share of 19.3% (95% CI 16.3–22.7; n=114/591) versus 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally is less likely to be a recording artefact. `cty:no_treatment_record:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, cty:curative:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`
- Uncertainty remains around case-mix because stage at diagnosis is missing for ~48% of new patients nationally, preventing stage adjustment of crude county shares, and while the digitised age-adjusted curative share for men (17.9%, 95% CI 13.9–22.9, ±0.5 percentage points) excludes the 26.7% national reference, the estimate for women (22.6%, 95% CI 16.8–29.3, ±0.5 percentage points) has a 95% confidence interval that includes the 26.9% national reference. `bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, k16:curative:men:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:women:national`
- Public registry data also cannot show where pathway delays occur in Kymenlaakson hyvinvointialue because symptom onset, primary care and occupational health visits, referral dates, imaging waiting times, and the dates of specialist visits and treatment start are not captured in county-level public reporting. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Preliminary, unpublished figures report that lung cancer patients in Finland wait 155 days on average from symptoms to treatment—comprising 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care—and because Finland lacks a national cancer quality registry or coordinated pathway monitoring, local managers cannot see from public sources which of these stages drives late presentation in the county. `mot:total_fi, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, filha:registry, q:47c7e11303, q:4e702a20fe`

## What to do first

- Because the Health Care Act sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and national target times and diagnostic time limits are still under preparation, Kymenlaakson hyvinvointialue should begin tracking local access times across referral, specialist assessment, diagnosis, and treatment start using the THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance definitions for 16 cancers and flagging stalled handovers. `law:no_max, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:cd51b9d5b6, q:8f358a9de6, q:def306014c`
- Drawing on national policy measures for standardised referral criteria and practices identified in reviewed regional pathway documents, the county should adopt a structured referral template, instruct primary care to refer patients with persistent unexplained symptoms for a pulmonologist assessment even when an X-ray is normal, and assign a named care coordinator for the diagnostic phase, as mentor insight indicates that a weak referral receives a lower urgency rating in specialist care. `q:37bb4c3b38, q:29b792498e, q:9a1cd194f5, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, reg:named_contact, q:fc81032a80, q:74a8cee731, mentor:kaisa_referral`
- Finally, while Kymenlaakson hyvinvointialue's 16.4% (95% CI 13.7–19.6; n=97/591) no-treatment-record share versus 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally suggests treatment capture is relatively complete, the county should audit and standardise HILMO MDT coding, currently at 0.5% (95% CI 0.2–1.5; n=3/591) versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, and stage recording at diagnosis (~48% missing nationally) in line with national plans for uniform recording instructions and a cancer quality registry targeting 90% coverage. `cty:no_treatment_record:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, cty:mdt_recorded:Kymenlaakson_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:990a0d39a0, q:4c7a01d84c, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695`

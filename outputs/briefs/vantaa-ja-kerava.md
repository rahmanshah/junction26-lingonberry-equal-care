## Where do we lag?

**Categories overlap: Vantaa ja Kerava lags in crude life-prolonging treatment (20.3%, 95% CI 17.3–23.8 vs 30.4%) and crude MDT recording (0.2%, 95% CI 0.0–0.9 vs 6.0%).** `cty:life_prolonging:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:mdt_recorded:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded`

- The county's crude life-prolonging treatment share is 20.3% (95% CI 17.3–23.8; n=121/595) alongside the national 30.4% (4522/14873), and the 95% confidence interval excludes the national value. `cty:life_prolonging:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging`
- An MDT meeting is recorded for a crude 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–0.9; n=1/595) of county patients alongside 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value, though MDT records measure recording practice rather than care because HILMO captures procedures only from direct patient contacts. `cty:mdt_recorded:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- Because treatment categories overlap (a patient can have several), the county's crude curative treatment share is 35.6% (95% CI 31.9–39.6; n=212/595) versus 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally (95% confidence interval excludes the national value), while crude symptomatic treatment is 36.1% (95% CI 32.4–40.1; n=215/595) versus 39.5% (5871/14873) nationally (95% confidence interval includes the national value). `cty:curative:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:symptomatic:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- Additionally, a crude 28.9% (95% CI 25.4–32.7; n=172/595) of county patients have no treatment record within 12 months versus 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally—the 95% confidence interval includes the national value, making it not distinguishable—and this category mixes untreated patients and treatment that was not recorded. `cty:no_treatment_record:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

## How sure are we?

**County treatment shares are crude, lacking age adjustment and stage adjustment, while public registry data omit pathway timestamps from symptom onset and primary care through treatment start.** `bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- The county shares are crude and have separate limitations: (a) they are not age-adjusted (age-adjusted values exist only from Kuva 16 for curative treatment by sex, with digitised national reference lines at ±0.5 percentage points of 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women); (b) they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- In a journal article, hospital discharge register (HILMO) diagnoses matched the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, but this concerns diagnosis concordance only and says nothing about how completely treatment, referrals or MDT meetings are recorded, while national lung cancer data reporting and storage remain incomplete. `lit:hilmo_90, q:950a2f797b`
- Public report data omit symptom onset, referral dates, initial specialist visit dates (93.6% have a HILMO visit within 12 months; visits up to 3 months before diagnosis set follow-up start) and treatment start dates; primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is unmentioned, and imaging is tracked only indirectly via clinical-only diagnoses (about 20%). `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc`
- Preliminary, unpublished figures from a national study indicate that lung cancer care in Finland takes 155 days on average from symptoms to treatment (56 days patient delay, 41 days in primary care and 58 days in specialist care) versus 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark, though these preliminary averages are national rather than county-specific. `mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing stage, drug treatment and MDT coding figures, reporting access times using THL 21/2016 definitions with handover alerts, and standardising referrals and diagnostic coordination.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, thl:21_2016, q:950a2f797b`

- Given the county's crude 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–0.9; n=1/595) MDT recording versus 6.0% (893/14873) and crude 20.3% (95% CI 17.3–23.8; n=121/595) life-prolonging share versus 30.4% (4522/14873), as categories overlap, the team recommends publishing per-county figures on stage (missing for about 48%), drug treatment and MDT coding as data-quality signals while Finland lacks a quality registry. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, cty:life_prolonging:Vantaan_ja_Keravan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:bfa4196bfd, q:950a2f797b, q:82d365cd2c, filha:registry, q:47c7e11303`
- Because the Health Care Act sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and Finland lacks uniform diagnostic timetables, the team recommends reporting access times using THL guidance 21/2016 definitions (for 16 cancers) with handover alerts; the Filha National Lung Cancer Programme's diagnostics recommendation with time limits is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, law:no_max, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:ccda20dce9, q:20d6ca414e, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2`
- Backed by mentor insight that a weak referral gets a lower urgency rating in specialist care, the team recommends a standard referral template, a rule to refer persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray to a pulmonologist, and a named diagnostic-phase contact; the STM roadmap (measure 1.2.3) plans care-coordinator guidance by 2026–2028 that counties adopt by 2029–2031. `rec:2, mentor:kaisa_referral, q:556431a0c2, q:1b9eaf2217, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`

## Where do we lag?

**In Etelä-Karjala, crude recorded shares for curative, life-prolonging, and symptomatic care and MDT meetings lag national benchmarks, while the crude no-treatment-record share is higher.** `cty:curative:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:symptomatic:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, nat:life_prolonging, nat:symptomatic, nat:no_treatment_record, nat:mdt_recorded`

- Across overlapping treatment categories where a patient can receive several treatments, Etelä-Karjala's crude shares are 18.9% (95% CI 15.1–23.3; n=67/355) vs 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally for curative treatment, 21.4% (95% CI 17.5–26.0; n=76/355) vs 30.4% (4522/14873) for life-prolonging treatment, and 31.3% (95% CI 26.7–36.3; n=111/355) vs 39.5% (5871/14873) for symptomatic treatment, with every 95% confidence interval excluding the national value. `cty:curative:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- In figures digitised from Kuva 16 (precision ±0.5 percentage points; curative treatment only, by sex), the age-adjusted curative share gap holds for men at 18.9% (95% CI 13.5–25.7) vs 26.7% nationally (95% confidence interval excludes the national value), whereas for women at 19.8% (95% CI 13.4–29.0) vs 26.9% nationally the 95% confidence interval includes the national value. `k16:curative:men:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:women:national`
- The crude share of patients with no treatment record within 12 months is 42.8% (95% CI 37.8–48.0; n=152/355) vs 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally, and the crude share with a recorded MDT meeting is 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–1.1; n=0/355) vs 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with each 95% confidence interval excluding the national value. `cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded`

## How sure are we?

**County shares are crude and unadjusted for stage, the high crude no-treatment-record share suggests low treatment shares may partly reflect unrecorded care, and public data omit pathway timestamps.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, bs:coverage:Referral`

- Because Etelä-Karjala's crude share with no treatment record within 12 months of 42.8% (95% CI 37.8–48.0; n=152/355) exceeds the national 27.5% (4089/14873)—a category mixing untreated patients and unrecorded treatment—we infer as a data-quality signal that low crude treatment shares may partly reflect unrecorded treatment. `cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- The county shares are crude (not adjusted for age or stage) because stage at diagnosis is missing for ~48% of new patients nationally, a journal article's finding of about 90% HILMO-to-Cancer Registry concordance concerns diagnoses only rather than treatment recording, and HILMO MDT entries reflect recording practice from direct patient contacts rather than care. `bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, lit:hilmo_90, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- Public report data do not measure symptom onset, primary care visits (which are not included in the report's data), occupational health, referral dates, imaging waiting times, or the dates of initial specialist visits and treatment start, even though a lung cancer visit appears in HILMO within 12 months for 93.6% of patients nationally. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Preliminary, unpublished national study figures report an average of 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland (vs 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark), including 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care, while mentor insights note that occupational health speeds specialist access and weak referrals receive lower urgency ratings. `mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, mentor:hanna_occupational, mentor:kaisa_referral`

## What should we do first?

**Given Etelä-Karjala's high crude no-treatment-record share, the team recommends prioritising recording-completeness reporting, alongside reporting THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance access times with handover alerts and standardising referrals and coordination.** `rec:3, rec:1, rec:2, cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, thl:21_2016, q:950a2f797b, q:556431a0c2`

- Given Etelä-Karjala's crude no-treatment-record share of 42.8% (95% CI 37.8–48.0; n=152/355) vs 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally and crude MDT-recorded share of 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–1.1; n=0/355) vs 6.0% (893/14873), the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment, and MDT coding primarily as data-quality signals while the roadmap plans a national quality registry targeting 90% coverage including stage. `rec:3, cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, q:950a2f797b, q:82d365cd2c, q:f07a34a300, q:f540c706f8`
- Because the Health Care Act (§52) sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and national target times are only planned—in the roadmap for 2026–2028 and in Filha's diagnostics recommendation before the 2030 interim evaluation—the team recommends reporting access times from referral to treatment start using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance definitions (covering 16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers. `rec:1, law:no_max, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:def306014c, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2`
- Because referrals follow local instructions, mentor insight notes weak referrals receive lower urgency, and roadmap plans for referral criteria (2026–2028) and care-coordinator guidance (2026–2028; county adoption 2029–2031) leave the care phase open, the team recommends a referral template and persistent-symptoms rule after a normal X-ray modelled on the KYS regional pathway document, plus a named diagnostic-phase contact. `rec:2, q:556431a0c2, mentor:kaisa_referral, q:9a1cd194f5, q:c61439b30f, q:050ee09ebe, q:abcbb06ae3, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent`

## How sure are we?

**Etelä-Karjala's crude shares are not age-adjusted and cannot be stage-adjusted because stage is missing for ~48% of patients, while public data omit key pathway dates and unrecorded treatment.** `bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit`

- The county treatment shares are crude and have separate limitations: they are not age-adjusted (age-adjusted values exist only from digitised Kuva 16 for curative treatment by sex, precision ±0.5 percentage points), and separately they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of new patients. `k16:curative:men:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:women:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- While a journal article found HILMO diagnoses matched the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, Etelä-Karjala's crude no-treatment-record share of 42.8% (95% CI 37.8–48.0; n=152/355) versus 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally has a 95% confidence interval excluding and above the national value, signaling that low crude shares across overlapping treatment categories may partly reflect unrecorded treatment. `lit:hilmo_90, cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Public report data do not measure symptoms or referrals, primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned, imaging appears only indirectly as clinical diagnosis basis in about 20% of patients, and records do not show when the first specialist visit or treatment started. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Preliminary, unpublished national figures from a Yle MOT study report an average of 155 days from first symptoms to treatment in Finland (56 days patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care) versus 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark, but no county-specific wait times exist. `mot:total_fi, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment and MDT meetings, reporting access times using THL 21/2016 definitions, and standardising referrals and diagnostic coordination.** `rec:3, rec:1, rec:2, thl:21_2016, q:950a2f797b, q:556431a0c2`

- Given Etelä-Karjala's crude no-treatment-record share of 42.8% (95% CI 37.8–48.0; n=152/355) versus 27.5% nationally, crude MDT share of 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–1.1; n=0/355) versus 6.0% (where treatment categories overlap and 95% confidence intervals exclude national values), and ~48% missing stage, the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment and MDT coding to address incomplete reporting. `rec:3, cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:950a2f797b`
- Because the Health Care Act (§52) sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and Finland lacks uniform national access-time monitoring, the team recommends reporting access times from referral to first specialist visit, diagnosis and treatment start using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance definitions (which cover 16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers. `rec:1, law:no_max, q:4e702a20fe, q:ccda20dce9, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4`
- Because referrals follow local instructions, treatment responsibility organisation varies regionally, and mentor insight notes that a weak referral gets a lower urgency rating in specialist care, the team recommends adopting a KYS-style referral content list and template, referring patients with persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray to a pulmonologist, and naming a diagnostic-phase contact person. `rec:2, q:556431a0c2, q:1b9eaf2217, mentor:kaisa_referral`
- The team's recommendations prepare for national plans: the STM roadmap plans pathway access times and coding practices (measure 3.1.2), referral criteria (measure 2.5.2), care-coordinator guidance (measure 1.2.3, adopted by 2029–2031) and a quality registry (measure 3.2.1, 90% coverage by 2029–2031) by 2026–2028, while the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, q:0c7bf2dacb, q:def306014c, q:c61439b30f, q:abcbb06ae3, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2`

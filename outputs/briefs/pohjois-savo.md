## Where do we lag?

**Across overlapping categories, Pohjois-Savo's crude recorded MDT share lags the national figure, while its crude curative treatment share is not distinguishable from the national benchmark.** `cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, cty:curative:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`

- In Pohjois-Savo, the crude curative treatment share is 24.5% (95% CI 21.3–28.0; n=157/641) compared with 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally, so the 95% confidence interval includes the national value. `cty:curative:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`
- Across overlapping treatment categories where a patient can have several treatments, Pohjois-Savo's crude shares exceed national values—with 95% confidence intervals excluding the national figure—for life-prolonging treatment at 38.7% (95% CI 35.0–42.5; n=248/641) versus 30.4% (4522/14873) nationally and symptomatic treatment at 51.2% (95% CI 47.3–55.0; n=328/641) versus 39.5% (5871/14873) nationally. `cty:life_prolonging:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- Pohjois-Savo's crude share of patients with an MDT meeting recorded is 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–0.9; n=1/641) versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value, though MDT records measure recording practice rather than care because HILMO captures specialist procedures only from direct patient contacts. `cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- At the national level (not county-specific), preliminary, unpublished figures show lung cancer care takes 155 days on average from symptoms to treatment in Finland—comprising 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care and 58 days in specialist care—compared with 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark. `mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## How sure are we?

**Public data support relatively complete treatment recording in Pohjois-Savo, but county shares are crude and key pathway stages remain unmeasured.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health`

- Pohjois-Savo's crude share with no treatment record within 12 months (mixing untreated patients and unrecorded treatment) is 19.3% (95% CI 16.5–22.6; n=124/641) versus 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally; a 95% confidence interval excluding and entirely below 27.5% is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording as an inference, not a finding, making a low crude curative share less likely to be only a recording artefact. `cty:no_treatment_record:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- County shares are crude and have separate limitations: (a) they are not age-adjusted (age-adjusted values exist only from Kuva 16 for curative treatment by sex, digitised from a figure at ±0.5 percentage points, with national reference lines of 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women); and (b) they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `cty:curative:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- A journal article found HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types (diagnosis concordance only), whereas Finland lacks a lung cancer quality registry, data reporting is incomplete, and the KYS regional pathway document states KYS reviews every patient in a weekly MDT despite Pohjois-Savo's 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–0.9; n=1/641) crude HILMO-recorded MDT share. `lit:hilmo_90, filha:registry, q:47c7e11303, q:950a2f797b, reg:mdt_practice, cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:MDT`
- Public Cancer Registry report data do not measure symptom onset or referral dates, do not include primary care visits or mention occupational health, capture imaging only indirectly as clinical-only diagnosis in about 20% of patients without wait times, and record whether a specialist visit (93.6% within 12 months) or treatment occurred within 12 months but not when. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing recording completeness for Pohjois-Savo's crude shares, reporting THL 21/2016 access times with handover alerts, and pairing KYS referral rules with a diagnostic contact.** `rec:3, rec:1, rec:2, cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, thl:21_2016, reg:kys_template, reg:named_contact`

- With Pohjois-Savo's crude recorded MDT share at 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–0.9; n=1/641) versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally despite weekly KYS regional-pathway MDT reviews, and stage missing for about 48% of patients, the team recommends publishing county stage, drug and MDT recording completeness; the STM roadmap (measure 3.2.1) plans a quality registry decree update by 2026–2028 and 90% stage coverage by 2029–2031. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, reg:mdt_practice, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:82d365cd2c, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:f540c706f8`
- Because the Health Care Act sets no maximum time for a cancer suspicion and Finland lacks national pathway monitoring, the team recommends reporting lung cancer access times (referral to specialist visit, diagnosis and treatment start) using THL 21/2016 definitions (covering 16 cancers) with handover alerts; the STM roadmap (measure 3.1.2) plans national pathways and access times by 2026–2028 and 2029–2031. `rec:1, law:no_max, q:4e702a20fe, q:ccda20dce9, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:def306014c, q:b326e35410`
- Because a weak referral gets a lower specialist urgency rating (mentor insight), the team recommends adopting a KYS-style referral template and primary-care rule to refer persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray, as in the KYS regional pathway document; the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation (referral criteria and time limits) is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:2, mentor:kaisa_referral, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, q:29b792498e, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2`
- Because regional variation in organising treatment responsibility is large and no reviewed regional pathway document names a diagnostic contact, the team recommends naming a contact from referral to diagnosis; the STM roadmap plans named care-coordinator guidance (measure 1.2.3, by 2026–2028; county adoption by 2029–2031, phase unspecified) and standardised referral criteria (measure 2.5.2, by 2026–2028 and 2029–2031). `rec:2, q:971b2f468f, q:1b9eaf2217, reg:named_contact, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe, q:37bb4c3b38, q:c61439b30f`

# Etelä-Savo: lung cancer pathway brief

Status: **validated** · gemini-4-argon (thinking HIGH) · 2026-11-14T12:58:06+00:00 · attempts: 1

## Where do we lag?

**Etelä-Savo's crude recorded MDT share is below national, its crude curative share is not distinguishable from national, and its overlapping crude life-prolonging and symptomatic shares are above national.** `cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, cty:curative:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`

- Etelä-Savo's crude curative treatment share is 23.4% (95% CI 19.8–27.6; n=105/448) alongside the national 27.0% (4009/14873); because the 95% confidence interval includes 27.0%, this crude share is not distinguishable from the national value. `cty:curative:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`
- Because treatment categories overlap and a patient can receive several, Etelä-Savo also has crude shares of 36.4% (95% CI 32.1–40.9; n=163/448) for life-prolonging treatment versus 30.4% (4522/14873) nationally and 46.2% (95% CI 41.6–50.8; n=207/448) for symptomatic treatment versus 39.5% (5871/14873) nationally, where each 95% confidence interval excludes and lies above the national value. `cty:life_prolonging:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- An MDT meeting is recorded for a crude 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–1.2; n=1/448) of Etelä-Savo patients versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding and falling below the national value, though MDT records measure recording practice rather than care because HILMO captures procedures only from direct patient contacts. `cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- Nationally, the Health Care Act (§52) sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion, while preliminary, unpublished national averages from an Yle MOT study report 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland (versus 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark), with 56 days patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care. `law:no_max, mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## How sure are we?

**Public registry data suggest relatively complete treatment recording in Etelä-Savo, but county shares are crude and pre-diagnosis pathway stages and access times remain unmeasured.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- No treatment record within 12 months mixes untreated patients and unrecorded treatment; Etelä-Savo's crude 23.2% share (95% CI 19.5–27.3; n=104/448) has a 95% confidence interval below and excluding the national 27.5% (4089/14873), which as an inference, not a finding, is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording, so a low curative share is less likely to be only a recording artefact. `cty:no_treatment_record:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- County shares are crude with separate limitations: they are not age-adjusted—an age-adjusted value exists only from Kuva 16 for curative treatment by sex, where the reference lines digitised from the figure at about ±0.5 percentage points are 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women—and, separately, they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- While a 2019 journal article found HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, this concerns diagnosis concordance only—not treatment, referral, or MDT recording—and public data do not measure symptom onset, referrals, county specialist visit waits, or when treatment started. `lit:hilmo_90, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Outpatient primary care visits are not included in the report's data and occupational health is not mentioned at all, while imaging appears only indirectly as diagnosis based on clinical examination alone in about 20% of patients rather than imaging dates or waits. `bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing stage, drug, and MDT recording completeness, reporting THL 21/2016 access times with handover alerts, and adopting a KYS-style referral template and diagnostic contact.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, thl:21_2016, reg:kys_template, reg:named_contact, q:950a2f797b`

- The team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment, and MDT coding as data-quality signals, given Etelä-Savo's crude 0.2% (95% CI 0.0–1.2; n=1/448) recorded MDT share versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, about 48% missing stage, incomplete data requiring uniform instructions, and STM roadmap measure 3.2.1 planning a quality registry decree update by 2026–2028 and 90% stage coverage by 2029–2031. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Etelä_Savon_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:950a2f797b, q:4c7a01d84c, q:82d365cd2c, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:f540c706f8`
- The team recommends reporting access times using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 definitions (16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers while national monitoring is lacking: STM roadmap measure 3.1.2 plans national pathway descriptions and access times by 2026–2028 (all significant cancers by 2029–2031), and the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation with time limits is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:cd51b9d5b6, q:4e702a20fe, q:ccda20dce9, q:ed87906241, q:def306014c, q:b326e35410, q:8c9c032c25, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2`
- The team also recommends adopting a KYS-style referral template, a rule to refer persistent unexplained symptoms to a pulmonologist after a normal X-ray, and a named diagnostic-phase contact: among reviewed regional pathway documents, only the KYS pathway provides this template and X-ray rule, no reviewed pathway names a diagnostic contact, and mentor insight notes weak referrals get lower specialist urgency. `rec:2, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, reg:named_contact, mentor:kaisa_referral`
- The team recommends these local steps while national plans proceed: STM roadmap measure 2.5.2 plans standardised referral criteria and symptom-based models by 2026–2028, STM roadmap measure 1.2.3 plans care-coordinator guidance by 2026–2028 for county adoption by 2029–2031, and the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation with primary-care referral criteria is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:2, q:556431a0c2, q:82b59905ad, q:0780bf74b0, q:37bb4c3b38, q:c61439b30f, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:29b792498e, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2`

## Where do we lag?

**In Päijät-Häme, the crude recorded multidisciplinary team share of 0.3% lags the 6.0% national share, while the crude curative share of 29.6% is not distinguishable from 27.0% nationally.** `cty:mdt_recorded:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, cty:curative:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`

- Päijät-Häme's crude share of patients with a multidisciplinary team meeting recorded is 0.3% (95% CI 0.1–1.1; n=2/659), whose 95% confidence interval excludes the national 6.0% (893/14873), though HILMO records only procedures from direct patient contacts and measures recording practice rather than care. `cty:mdt_recorded:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- The county's crude curative treatment share is 29.6% (95% CI 26.2–33.2; n=195/659), with a 95% confidence interval that includes the national 27.0% (4009/14873), while crude life-prolonging treatment at 44.5% (95% CI 40.7–48.3; n=293/659) and symptomatic treatment at 50.8% (95% CI 47.0–54.6; n=335/659) have 95% confidence intervals that exclude the national 30.4% (4522/14873) and 39.5% (5871/14873). `cty:curative:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- The crude share with no treatment record within 12 months—mixing untreated patients and unrecorded treatment—is 17.8% (95% CI 15.0–20.9; n=117/659), whose 95% confidence interval excludes the national 27.5% (4089/14873); as an inference, this data-quality signal is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording rather than unrecorded care. `cty:no_treatment_record:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- While public data do not measure county waiting times, preliminary national averages show 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland versus 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark, split into 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care and 58 days in specialist care. `bs:coverage:Treatment_start, mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## How sure are we?

**Public registry data support diagnosis concordance and crude county treatment shares, but cannot show stage-adjusted differences, primary care visits, referral dates or waiting times to treatment start.** `lit:hilmo_90, cty:curative:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- A journal article found that HILMO diagnoses matched the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, but Päijät-Häme's treatment shares are crude—not adjusted for age or stage—and stage at diagnosis is missing for ~48% of new patients nationally. `lit:hilmo_90, cty:curative:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- While the 95% confidence intervals for Päijät-Häme's crude shares exclude national values for life-prolonging (44.5% vs 30.4%), symptomatic (50.8% vs 39.5%), no-treatment-record (17.8% vs 27.5%) and MDT-recorded (0.3% vs 6.0%) categories, only national age-adjusted curative reference lines of 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women (digitised from a figure, ±0.5 percentage points) are available here. `cty:life_prolonging:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic, cty:no_treatment_record:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, cty:mdt_recorded:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national`
- Public data do not measure symptom onset or referrals, outpatient primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned at all, and imaging appears only indirectly as the basis of diagnosis in approximately 20% of cases without dates or waits. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc`
- In specialist care, public data show whether a lung cancer visit appears in HILMO within 12 months (93.6% nationally) and which treatment type is recorded within 12 months, but cannot show county visit shares or the dates when specialist visits or treatments started. `bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing county recording completeness, tracking access times under THL 21/2016 with handover alerts, and standardising referrals with a persistent-symptoms rule and named diagnostic contact.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, thl:21_2016, q:950a2f797b, q:9a1cd194f5`

- Because Päijät-Häme's crude recorded MDT share is 0.3% (95% CI 0.1–1.1; n=2/659) versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally and stage is missing for ~48% of patients nationally, the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment and MDT coding while a national quality registry aiming for 90% coverage is planned. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Päijät_Hämeen_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:950a2f797b, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695`
- Since the Health Care Act sets no maximum time for a cancer suspicion and national pathways with target access times are planned for 2026–2028, the team recommends reporting referral-to-treatment access times using THL guidance Ohjaus 21/2016 definitions (covering 16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers. `rec:1, law:no_max, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:ed87906241, q:def306014c, q:4e702a20fe`
- Given preliminary national delays of 41 days in primary care and 58 days in specialist care, plus a mentor insight that weak referrals receive lower urgency ratings, the team recommends a standard referral template, referring persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray to a pulmonologist, and naming a diagnostic contact person. `rec:2, mot:primary, mot:specialist, mentor:kaisa_referral, q:556431a0c2`
- The team recommends these local steps now because the national roadmap plans standardised referral criteria, symptom-based models and care-coordinator guidance for 2026–2028 (with county adoption in 2029–2031), and the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation with referral criteria and specialist time limits is due before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, rec:2, q:9a1cd194f5, q:c61439b30f, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2`

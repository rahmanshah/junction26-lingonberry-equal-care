# Pirkanmaa: lung cancer pathway brief

Status: **validated** · gemini-4-argon (thinking HIGH) · 2026-11-14T13:56:51+00:00 · attempts: 2

## Where do we lag?

**Pirkanmaa's overlapping crude treatment shares exceed national values, so its pathway gaps are unmonitored times and missing diagnostic contacts in regional pathway documents rather than low recorded treatment.** `cty:curative:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, cty:symptomatic:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, reg:not_monitored, reg:named_contact`

- In Pirkanmaa, the crude treatment shares—whose categories overlap because a patient can have several—have 95% confidence intervals that exclude and exceed national values: curative is 31.9% (95% CI 29.4–34.6; n=400/1253) versus 27.0% (4009/14873), life-prolonging is 36.2% (95% CI 33.5–38.9; n=453/1253) versus 30.4% (4522/14873), and symptomatic is 45.0% (95% CI 42.3–47.8; n=564/1253) versus 39.5% (5871/14873). `cty:curative:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- In these tables where the categories overlap, Pirkanmaa's crude share with no treatment record within 12 months is 17.9% (95% CI 15.8–20.1; n=224/1253), with a 95% confidence interval excluding and below the national 27.5% (4089/14873), and its crude recorded MDT share is 37.4% (95% CI 34.8–40.1; n=469/1253), excluding and above the national 6.0% (893/14873). `cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, cty:mdt_recorded:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded`
- In regional pathway documents, Pirkanmaa states target or typical times of 1–2 working days for referral handling, 3–4 working days for invitations, and 3–5 days for bronchoscopy results, yet no region reports whether it meets its stated times or names a contact person for the diagnostic phase. `reg:pirha_referral, reg:pirha_bronch, reg:not_monitored, reg:named_contact`
- The Health Care Act sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion, and preliminary, unpublished national averages show lung cancer takes 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland versus 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark, comprising 56 days patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care. `law:no_max, mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## How sure are we?

**Pirkanmaa's low crude no-treatment-record share suggests relatively complete treatment recording, but county figures are unadjusted for age or stage and public data omit pathway waiting times.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- No treatment record within 12 months mixes untreated patients and unrecorded treatment; because Pirkanmaa's crude 17.9% share (95% CI 15.8–20.1; n=224/1253) has a 95% confidence interval entirely below the national 27.5% (4089/14873), this is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording as an inference, not a finding, whereas MDT records measure recording practice, not care. `bs:coverage:Treatment_start, cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- County shares in Taulukko 11 are crude and have separate limitations: they are not age-adjusted—age-adjusted county shares appear only in Kuva 16, where digitised national curative lines are 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women (±0.5 percentage points) without a digitised Pirkanmaa figure—and separately, they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- A journal article found HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, which concerns diagnosis concordance only, whereas HILMO captures specialist procedures only from direct patient contacts and lung cancer data reporting, collection, and storage remain incomplete. `lit:hilmo_90, q:39e6ba43a9, q:950a2f797b`
- In public data, primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned, imaging appears only indirectly as clinical diagnosis alone in about 20% of patients, and symptom onset, referral dates, specialist visit timing, and treatment start dates are unmeasured. `bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends reporting access times using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance, standardising referrals and diagnostic contacts from regional pathway documents, and publishing stage, drug, and MDT recording completeness.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, thl:21_2016, reg:not_monitored, reg:named_contact, bs:coverage:Diagnosis`

- Because Pirkanmaa's regional pathway document states target times of 1–2 working days for referral handling, 3–4 working days for invitations, and 3–5 days for bronchoscopy results without reporting whether they are met, the team recommends reporting access times using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance definitions for 16 cancers and flagging stalled handovers. `reg:pirha_referral, reg:pirha_bronch, reg:not_monitored, rec:1, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4`
- As the Filha National Lung Cancer Programme's diagnostics recommendation with primary-care referral criteria and recommended time limits is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation, the team recommends adopting a standard referral content list and template and a rule to refer persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray, supported by mentor insight that weak referrals get lower urgency ratings. `q:8f358a9de6, q:29b792498e, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2, rec:2, mentor:kaisa_referral`
- Because no region names a diagnostic-phase contact person in regional pathway documents and STM roadmap measure 1.2.3 plans guidance on a named care-coordinator model by 2026–2028 for counties to adopt by 2029–2031 while leaving the care phase open, the team recommends naming a contact person from referral to diagnosis. `reg:named_contact, q:fc81032a80, q:050ee09ebe, q:abcbb06ae3, rec:2`
- Although Pirkanmaa's crude 17.9% (95% CI 15.8–20.1; n=224/1253) no-treatment-record share is below the national 27.5% (4089/14873), stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients and lung cancer data reporting is incomplete without a quality registry, so the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment, and MDT coding. `cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:950a2f797b, q:47c7e11303, rec:3`

## Where do we lag?

**Pirkanmaa's crude treatment shares exceed national values, so its main pathway gaps lie in unmonitored local target times and unmeasured access times from referral to treatment start.** `cty:curative:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, cty:symptomatic:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, nat:life_prolonging, nat:symptomatic, reg:not_monitored, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- Across overlapping treatment categories (a patient may receive several), Pirkanmaa's crude shares are above national values, and each 95% confidence interval excludes the national value: curative 31.9% (95% CI 29.4–34.6; n=400/1253) versus 27.0% (4009/14873), life-prolonging 36.2% (95% CI 33.5–38.9; n=453/1253) versus 30.4% (4522/14873), and symptomatic 45.0% (95% CI 42.3–47.8; n=564/1253) versus 39.5% (5871/14873). `cty:curative:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- Pirkanmaa's crude share with no treatment record within 12 months (which mixes patients receiving no treatment and unrecorded treatment) is 17.9% (95% CI 15.8–20.1; n=224/1253), whose 95% confidence interval excludes the national 27.5% (4089/14873), while its crude MDT-recorded share is 37.4% (95% CI 34.8–40.1; n=469/1253), excluding the national 6.0% (893/14873). `cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, cty:mdt_recorded:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded`
- Pirkanmaa's regional pathway document states typical times of 1–2 working days to handle referrals, 3–4 working days for invitations, most often within a week for CT, and 3–5 days for bronchoscopy results, without reporting if targets are met or naming a diagnostic contact person, and the Health Care Act (§52) sets no maximum time for cancer suspicion. `reg:pirha_referral, reg:pirha_ct, reg:pirha_bronch, reg:not_monitored, reg:named_contact, law:no_max`
- Nationally, diagnostic speed varies widely without a uniform timetable, and preliminary, unpublished national figures from MOT show an average of 155 days from first symptoms to treatment in Finland (56 days patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care), compared with 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark. `q:971b2f468f, q:20d6ca414e, mot:total_fi, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk`

## How sure are we?

**Pirkanmaa's low crude share without a treatment record suggests relatively complete treatment recording, but public data lack stage for nearly half of patients and pathway wait times.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, q:7885b0974f, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- Reading the no-treatment-record share as a data-quality signal, we infer that Pirkanmaa's crude share of 17.9% (95% CI 15.8–20.1; n=224/1253) below the national 27.5% (4089/14873) is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording rather than direct evidence of it, whereas a journal article found about 90% HILMO-to-Cancer-Registry concordance for diagnoses only, saying nothing about treatment, referral, or MDT recording. `cty:no_treatment_record:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, lit:hilmo_90`
- County treatment shares are crude—not adjusted for age or stage—and cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for nearly half of new patients (about 48%), while Kuva 16 provides only sex-specific age-adjusted curative national reference lines (26.7% for men and 26.9% for women, digitised from a figure with ±0.5 percentage points precision). `bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national`
- Although Pirkanmaa's crude MDT-recorded share of 37.4% (95% CI 34.8–40.1; n=469/1253) exceeds the national 6.0% (893/14873), HILMO captures specialised care procedures (codes WZC15/WZC20) only from direct patient contacts, so MDT figures measure recording practice rather than care and do not comprehensively describe the extent of multidisciplinary review. `cty:mdt_recorded:Pirkanmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9, q:bfa4196bfd`
- Public data do not measure symptom onset or referrals, outpatient primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned, imaging appears only as the diagnosis basis in approximately 20% of cases without wait times, and records show whether a specialist visit (93.6%) or treatment occurred within 12 months, not when. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends that Pirkanmaa report THL 21/2016 access times with handover alerts, standardize referral criteria with a diagnostic contact person, and publish county recording completeness.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, thl:21_2016, reg:not_monitored, reg:named_contact, q:950a2f797b`

- Because Pirkanmaa's regional pathway document does not report whether its stated times are met and national monitoring is uncoordinated, the team recommends reporting access times (referral, first specialist visit, diagnosis, treatment start) using THL guidance 21/2016 definitions for 16 cancers with handover alerts while the roadmap plans national pathways by 2026–2028 and Filha's diagnostics guideline is due before 2030. `rec:1, reg:not_monitored, q:4e702a20fe, q:ccda20dce9, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:def306014c, q:b326e35410, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2, q:432c0e51d6`
- Because mentor insight notes that a weak referral gets a lower urgency rating in specialist care and regional pathway documents name no diagnostic contact person, the team recommends a standard referral template, a primary-care rule to refer persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray, and a named diagnostic contact while national referral criteria and coordinator guidance are planned for 2026–2028. `rec:2, mentor:kaisa_referral, reg:named_contact, q:556431a0c2, q:1b9eaf2217, q:37bb4c3b38, q:29b792498e, q:0780bf74b0, q:c61439b30f, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`
- Given that stage is missing for nearly half of new patients (about 48%), HILMO MDT codes do not comprehensively capture multidisciplinary review, and data reporting is deficient without a quality registry, the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment, and MDT coding while a national quality registry targeting 90% coverage and uniform recording instructions are planned. `rec:3, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9, q:bfa4196bfd, q:950a2f797b, q:82d365cd2c, filha:registry, q:47c7e11303, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:f540c706f8, q:990a0d39a0`

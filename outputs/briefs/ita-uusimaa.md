## Where do we lag?

**Itä-Uusimaa's crude recorded MDT meeting share is 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–1.4; n=0/272), where the 95% confidence interval excludes the national value of 6.0% (893/14873).** `cty:mdt_recorded:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded`

- This crude 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–1.4; n=0/272) MDT figure versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally measures recording practice rather than care, because HILMO captures specialised care procedures (codes WZC15/WZC20) only from direct patient contacts and does not comprehensively describe multidisciplinary review. `cty:mdt_recorded:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- The crude share with no treatment record within 12 months is 31.6% (95% CI 26.4–37.4; n=86/272) alongside 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally; its 95% confidence interval includes the national value, and this category mixes patients who received no treatment and treatment that was not recorded. `cty:no_treatment_record:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Across overlapping treatment categories where a patient may receive several types, crude shares are 28.3% (95% CI 23.3–33.9; n=77/272) versus 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally for curative, 30.1% (95% CI 25.0–35.9; n=82/272) versus 30.4% (4522/14873) for life-prolonging, and 36.8% (95% CI 31.3–42.6; n=100/272) versus 39.5% (5871/14873) for symptomatic treatment, with every 95% confidence interval including the national value. `cty:curative:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- Although county waiting times are not measured in public report data, preliminary national averages show 155 days from first symptoms to first treatment in Finland (compared with 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark), spanning 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## How sure are we?

**Diagnosis records match well nationally, but Itä-Uusimaa's crude treatment shares lack age or stage adjustment, and public data omit primary care, referrals, and treatment start dates.** `lit:hilmo_90, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- A journal article found HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types (diagnosis concordance only), whereas reading Itä-Uusimaa's above-national crude 31.6% (95% CI 26.4–37.4; n=86/272) no-treatment-record share versus 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally as a data-quality signal suggests—as an inference, not a finding—that lower recorded treatment shares may partly reflect unrecorded treatment. `lit:hilmo_90, cty:no_treatment_record:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- County shares are crude (not adjusted for age or stage): stage at diagnosis is missing for ~48% of new patients nationally, and while Kuva 16 gives digitised national age-adjusted curative lines (±0.5 percentage points precision) of 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women, Itä-Uusimaa's crude 28.3% (95% CI 23.3–33.9; n=77/272) curative share versus 27.0% (4009/14873) is unadjusted. `bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, cty:curative:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`
- In public data, primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned, symptoms and referrals are unmeasured, imaging appears only indirectly as the diagnosis basis in approximately 20% of cases, and HILMO records whether a specialist visit (93.6% nationally) or treatment occurred within 12 months but not when. `bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Nationally, Finland has no lung cancer or other cancer quality registry, and the Filha programme notes that reporting, collection, and storage of lung cancer data are currently incomplete, though the Cancer Registry, THL, and FICAN are assessing a quality registry starting with lung cancer. `filha:registry, q:47c7e11303, q:950a2f797b, q:e89c30a5a4`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing stage, drug treatment, and MDT recording completeness first, reporting access times using THL 21/2016 definitions with handover alerts, and standardising referrals and diagnostic contact.** `rec:3, rec:1, rec:2, thl:21_2016, q:950a2f797b`

- First, the team recommends publishing per-county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment, and MDT coding and reading gaps as data-quality signals first, given Itä-Uusimaa's crude 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–1.4; n=0/272) MDT share versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, its crude 31.6% (95% CI 26.4–37.4; n=86/272) no-treatment-record share versus 27.5% (4089/14873), and a planned national quality registry targeting 90% coverage including stage. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, cty:no_treatment_record:Itä_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:82d365cd2c`
- Next, because Health Care Act §52 sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and Finland lacks a unified national pathway monitoring system, the team recommends reporting referral-to-treatment access times using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance definitions (for 16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers while the roadmap plans national pathways and target access times by 2026–2028. `rec:1, law:no_max, q:4e702a20fe, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:def306014c, q:b326e35410`
- Finally, the team recommends a KYS-style referral template, pulmonologist referral for persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray, and a named diagnostic-phase contact, since referrals follow local instructions, mentor insight notes weak referrals get lower urgency ratings, Filha's diagnostics recommendation is due before the 2030 mid-term evaluation, and the roadmap plans coordinator guidance in 2026–2028 for 2029–2031 adoption. `rec:2, q:556431a0c2, mentor:kaisa_referral, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`

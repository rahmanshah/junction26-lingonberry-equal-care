## Where do we lag?

**In Keski-Uusimaa, the crude recorded multidisciplinary team share lags at 0.0% versus 6.0% nationally, whereas the crude curative share across overlapping treatment categories is 33.7% versus 27.0% nationally.** `cty:mdt_recorded:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, cty:curative:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`

- For multidisciplinary team meetings, Keski-Uusimaa's crude recorded share is 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–0.8; n=0/481) compared with 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value, though MDT records measure recording practice rather than care because HILMO captures specialist procedures only from direct patient contacts. `cty:mdt_recorded:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`
- Keski-Uusimaa does not lag in curative care: its crude curative treatment share is 33.7% (95% CI 29.6–38.0; n=162/481) compared with 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally, and the 95% confidence interval excludes the national value. `cty:curative:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`
- In the other overlapping treatment categories, where a patient can receive several treatments, the crude life-prolonging share is 28.9% (95% CI 25.0–33.1; n=139/481) versus 30.4% (4522/14873) nationally and the crude symptomatic share is 39.5% (95% CI 35.2–43.9; n=190/481) versus 39.5% (5871/14873) nationally, and each 95% confidence interval includes the national value. `cty:life_prolonging:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- The crude share with no treatment record within 12 months is 26.4% (95% CI 22.7–30.5; n=127/481) compared with 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally, a category mixing untreated patients and unrecorded treatment whose 95% confidence interval includes the national value. `cty:no_treatment_record:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

## How sure are we?

**Diagnosis concordance in HILMO is about 90% for most cancers, but county treatment shares are crude and public data omit key pathway stages and timestamps.** `lit:hilmo_90, fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- Taulukko 11 gives crude county shares that are not age-adjusted, as county-level age-adjusted shares appear only in Kuva 16 alongside digitised national curative reference lines of 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women (±0.5 percentage points), and separately they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- A journal article found about 90% diagnosis concordance between HILMO and the Cancer Registry for most cancer types, which concerns diagnoses only, whereas public report data do not measure symptoms or referrals, are not including primary care visits in the report's data, do not mention occupational health, and record specialist visits (93.6%) and treatments without start dates. `lit:hilmo_90, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Public data also lack imaging wait times, noting only that about 20% of diagnoses rest on clinical examination such as imaging alone, while preliminary unpublished national averages—not county figures—show 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland versus 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark, including 56 patient, 41 primary care, and 58 specialist care days. `bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends publishing how stage, drug therapy, and MDT meetings are coded, reporting THL 21/2016 access times with handover alerts, and standardising referrals and diagnostic-phase coordination.** `rec:3, rec:1, rec:2`

- Given Keski-Uusimaa's 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–0.8; n=0/481) crude recorded MDT share versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally and stage missing for about 48% of patients, the team recommends publishing county coding coverage for stage, drug therapy, and MDT meetings to address deficient data reporting and ensure uniform recording instructions. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Keski_Uudenmaan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:950a2f797b, q:4c7a01d84c, q:82d365cd2c`
- Because the Health Care Act §52 sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and national access-time monitoring is lacking, the team recommends reporting access times from referral to treatment start using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 definitions for 16 cancers and flagging stalled handovers. `rec:1, law:no_max, q:4e702a20fe, q:ccda20dce9, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4`
- The team recommends adopting a referral template modelled on KYS's regional pathway document, referring persistent unexplained symptoms to a pulmonologist after a normal X-ray, and naming a diagnostic-phase contact person, as mentor insight indicates weak referrals get lower urgency ratings and reviewed regional pathway documents lack a diagnostic-phase contact. `rec:2, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, reg:named_contact, mentor:kaisa_referral`
- The team's recommendations bridge the gap while the Filha programme's national diagnostics recommendation with primary-care referral criteria and recommended time limits is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation, and while the STM roadmap plans national pathways and referral criteria (measures 3.1.2 and 2.5.2) and care-coordinator guidance (measure 1.2.3) by 2026–2028 for county adoption by 2029–2031. `rec:1, rec:2, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2, q:432c0e51d6, q:ed87906241, q:def306014c, q:9a1cd194f5, q:c61439b30f, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`

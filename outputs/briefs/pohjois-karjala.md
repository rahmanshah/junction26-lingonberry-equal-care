# Pohjois-Karjala: lung cancer pathway brief

Status: **validated** · gemini-4-argon (thinking HIGH) · 2026-11-14T12:59:39+00:00 · attempts: 1

## Where do we lag?

**Pohjois-Karjala lags national benchmarks in crude curative and life-prolonging treatment shares and has a higher crude symptomatic share across overlapping treatment categories.** `cty:curative:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:symptomatic:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, nat:life_prolonging, nat:symptomatic`

- In overlapping treatment categories, Pohjois-Karjala's crude curative treatment share is 21.4% (95% CI 18.0–25.1; n=109/510) versus 27.0% (4009/14873) nationally, and its crude life-prolonging treatment share is 23.5% (95% CI 20.1–27.4; n=120/510) versus 30.4% (4522/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence intervals excluding the national values. `cty:curative:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging`
- The county's crude symptomatic treatment share is 52.2% (95% CI 47.8–56.5; n=266/510) compared with 39.5% (5871/14873) nationally, and the 95% confidence interval excludes the national value, stays above it, and reflects categories that overlap with other treatments. `cty:symptomatic:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- A multidisciplinary team (MDT) meeting is recorded for a crude share of 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–0.8; n=0/510) of patients in Pohjois-Karjala versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value, though MDT records measure recording practice rather than care because HILMO captures procedures only from direct patient contacts. `cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`

## How sure are we?

**Relatively complete treatment recording supports the low crude curative share across overlapping categories as an inference, though crude shares lack age and stage adjustment and public data omit timestamps.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, cty:curative:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- The crude share with no treatment record within 12 months (mixing untreated patients and unrecorded treatment) is 22.2% (95% CI 18.8–26.0; n=113/510), whose 95% confidence interval excludes and lies below the national 27.5% (4089/14873), which is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording, as an inference, not a finding, so the low crude curative share across overlapping categories is less likely to be only a recording artefact. `cty:no_treatment_record:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, cty:curative:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue`
- The county shares are crude and have separate limitations: they are not age-adjusted—digitised age-adjusted values from Kuva 16 (read from a figure at about ±0.5 percentage points) exist only for curative treatment by sex, with national reference lines at 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women—and, separately, they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- A journal article found HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types (diagnosis concordance only), while outpatient primary care visits are not included in the report's data and public data omit occupational health, symptoms, referral dates, imaging waits (clinical examination such as imaging is the diagnosis basis in about 20%) and specialist visit or treatment start dates (93.6% have a specialist visit within 12 months). `lit:hilmo_90, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Preliminary, unpublished national figures average 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland versus 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark—comprising 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care and 58 days in specialist care, the longest stage—and under the Health Care Act (§52) there is no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion. `mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, law:no_max`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends reporting THL 21/2016 access times with handover alerts, adopting KYS-style referral and diagnostic coordination rules, and publishing county recording completeness for stage, drugs and MDT.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3, thl:21_2016, reg:kys_template, q:950a2f797b`

- The team recommends reporting access times (referral to specialist visit, diagnosis and treatment start) using THL guidance Ohjaus 21/2016 (for 16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers; the STM roadmap (measure 3.1.2) plans national pathways and access times by 2026–2028, while the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation with time limits is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, thl:21_2016, q:ccda20dce9, q:24169121a4, q:def306014c, q:b326e35410, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2`
- To address referral quality—as a mentor insight notes a weak referral gets a lower urgency rating—the team recommends adopting the KYS care chain document's referral template and rule to refer persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray, plus naming a diagnostic contact person while the STM roadmap plans referral criteria (measure 2.5.2) and coordinator guidance (measure 1.2.3) by 2026–2028. `rec:2, mentor:kaisa_referral, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, reg:named_contact, q:c61439b30f, q:37bb4c3b38, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`
- Given Pohjois-Karjala's 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–0.8; n=0/510) crude recorded MDT share versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally and about 48% missing stage at diagnosis, the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment and MDT coding while the STM roadmap plans pathway coding rules (measure 3.1.2, by 2026–2028) and a quality registry with 90% coverage (measure 3.2.1, reports by 2029–2031). `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:950a2f797b, q:82d365cd2c, q:990a0d39a0, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:f540c706f8`

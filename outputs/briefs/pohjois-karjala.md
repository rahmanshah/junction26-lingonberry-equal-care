## How sure are we?

**Pohjois-Karjala's crude no-treatment-record share (22.2%, 95% CI 18.8–26.0 vs 27.5%) suggests relatively complete recording, but crude county shares lack age and stage adjustment and pathway timestamps.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- Because the 95% confidence interval for Pohjois-Karjala's crude no-treatment-record share (22.2%, 95% CI 18.8–26.0) lies entirely below the national 27.5%, this data-quality signal is consistent with relatively complete treatment recording as an inference, not a finding, so the low crude curative share (21.4%, 95% CI 18.0–25.1 vs 27.0%, where treatment categories overlap) is less likely to be only a recording artefact. `cty:no_treatment_record:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, cty:curative:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative`
- Taulukko 11 gives crude county shares with separate limitations: (a) they are not age-adjusted—county-level age-adjusted shares appear only in Kuva 16, where digitised national curative reference lines are 26.7% for men and 26.9% for women (±0.5 percentage points) without a digitised Pohjois-Karjala value—and (b) they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:national, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- A journal article found HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types (diagnosis concordance only), while primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned, symptom, referral, visit and treatment start dates are absent, and imaging appears only indirectly as clinical-only diagnosis in about 20% of patients. `lit:hilmo_90, bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Preliminary, unpublished national averages show lung cancer takes 155 days on average from symptoms to treatment in Finland (56 days patient delay, 41 days primary care, and 58 days in specialist care, the longest stage) versus 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark, while the Health Care Act sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion. `mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, law:no_max`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends auditing MDT and stage recording completeness, tracking access times with THL 21/2016 definitions, and adopting a KYS-style referral template, persistent-symptoms rule and diagnostic contact person.** `rec:1, rec:2, rec:3`

- Given Pohjois-Karjala's 0.0% (95% CI 0.0–0.8; n=0/510) crude recorded MDT share versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally and incomplete data collection (stage missing for about 48%), the team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment and MDT coding with uniform instructions, while the STM roadmap (measure 3.2.1) plans a national quality registry with a 90% data-coverage indicator including stage. `rec:3, cty:mdt_recorded:Pohjois_Karjalan_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, q:950a2f797b, q:4c7a01d84c, q:82d365cd2c, q:f07a34a300, q:4a856bd695, q:f540c706f8`
- Because Finland lacks unified access monitoring and diagnostic speed varies regionally, the team recommends reporting access times using THL Ohjaus 21/2016 guidance definitions with handover alerts; meanwhile, the STM roadmap (measure 3.1.2) plans national pathways with target access times for five cancers by 2026–2028, and the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:4e702a20fe, q:971b2f468f, q:ed87906241, q:def306014c, q:b326e35410, q:8f358a9de6, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2`
- Because referrals follow local guidelines and mentor insight indicates a weak referral gets a lower urgency rating, the team recommends a referral template and persistent-symptoms rule after a normal X-ray, as in the KYS care chain document, while the STM roadmap (measure 2.5.2) plans standardised referral criteria and symptom-based models for at least the five most common cancers by 2026–2028. `rec:2, mentor:kaisa_referral, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, q:556431a0c2, q:9a1cd194f5, q:c61439b30f, q:37bb4c3b38, q:0780bf74b0`
- Because reviewed regional pathway documents name no diagnostic-phase contact person, the team recommends naming a contact person from referral to diagnosis so patients know who holds the overall picture of their care; the STM roadmap (measure 1.2.3) plans guidance on a named care coordinator by 2026–2028 for counties to adopt by 2029–2031, leaving the target care phase open. `rec:2, reg:named_contact, q:74a8cee731, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`

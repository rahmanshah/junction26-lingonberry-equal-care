## Where do we lag?

**Varsinais-Suomi's crude, overlapping curative, life-prolonging and symptomatic treatment shares lag behind national values, while its crude share with no treatment record within 12 months is higher.** `cty:curative:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:symptomatic:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue`

- In overlapping treatment categories, crude county shares are below national values, as each 95% confidence interval excludes the national benchmark: curative treatment is 24.3% (95% CI 22.1–26.6; n=349/1437) versus 27.0% (4009/14873), life-prolonging is 9.7% (95% CI 8.3–11.4; n=140/1437) versus 30.4% (4522/14873), and symptomatic is 30.8% (95% CI 28.5–33.3; n=443/1437) versus 39.5% (5871/14873). `cty:curative:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`

## How sure are we?

**A journal article shows high diagnosis concordance, but Varsinais-Suomi's crude no-treatment-record share suggests low crude overlapping treatment shares partly reflect unrecorded treatment, and pathway timing data are missing.** `lit:hilmo_90, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Treatment_start, bs:coverage:Referral`

- A journal article indicates HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, which concerns diagnosis concordance only, whereas because Varsinais-Suomi's crude no-treatment-record share of 43.1% (95% CI 40.5–45.6) has a 95% CI above the 27.5% national value, low crude overlapping treatment shares may partly reflect unrecorded treatment. `lit:hilmo_90, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- The crude county shares (such as curative treatment at 24.3%, 95% CI 22.1–26.6) have separate limitations: they are not age-adjusted (an age-adjusted value exists only from Kuva 16 for curative treatment by sex), and separately they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `cty:curative:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:men:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis`
- Public report data do not measure symptoms or referrals, primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is not mentioned, imaging appears only indirectly as the diagnosis basis in about 20% of patients without wait times, and HILMO shows whether specialist visits (93.6%) and treatments occur within 12 months, not when. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Finland lacks a cancer quality registry, and only preliminary, unpublished national averages—not county figures—estimate the pathway from symptoms to treatment at 155 days in Finland (56 days patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care) versus 107 days in Sweden and 67 days in Denmark. `q:47c7e11303, mot:total_fi, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk`

## What should we do first?

**Given Varsinais-Suomi's 43.1% (95% CI 40.5–45.6) crude no-treatment-record share and unmeasured waits, the team recommends reporting recording completeness, tracking THL guidance 21/2016 access times, and standardising referrals and coordination.** `cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Referral, thl:21_2016, rec:3, rec:1, rec:2`

- The team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage, drug treatment and MDT meetings and reading gaps as data-quality signals, since in overlapping categories Varsinais-Suomi's crude no-treatment-record share is 43.1% (95% CI 40.5–45.6) and crude MDT share is 1.6% (95% CI 1.1–2.4), stage is missing for about 48% of patients nationally, and national lung cancer data reporting is incomplete. `rec:3, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:mdt_recorded:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:950a2f797b`
- Because the Health Care Act sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion and Finland lacks national pathway monitoring, the team recommends reporting access times from referral to specialist visit, diagnosis and treatment start using THL guidance Ohjaus 21/2016 definitions (covering 16 cancers) and flagging stalled handovers while the STM roadmap (measure 3.1.2) plans national pathways by 2026–2028. `rec:1, law:no_max, q:4e702a20fe, thl:21_2016, q:ed87906241, q:0c7bf2dacb`
- The team recommends a standard referral content list and template and referring persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray to a pulmonologist, since referrals follow local instructions, mentor insight notes weak referrals get lower urgency ratings, and the Filha programme's diagnostics recommendation with referral criteria is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:2, q:556431a0c2, mentor:kaisa_referral, q:8f358a9de6, q:614febebf2`
- Finally, the team recommends naming a contact person for the diagnostic phase from referral to diagnosis, as the STM roadmap (measure 1.2.3) plans guidance on a named care-coordinator model by 2026–2028 for counties to adopt by 2029–2031, but still maps out which stage of care to target. `rec:2, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:e31448e9c9`

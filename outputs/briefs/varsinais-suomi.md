# Varsinais-Suomi: lung cancer pathway brief

## Where do we lag?

**Varsinais-Suomi lags national benchmarks across overlapping crude treatment categories and crude multidisciplinary team recording, while a larger crude share of patients has no treatment record within 12 months.** `cty:curative:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:symptomatic:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:mdt_recorded:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue`

- In overlapping treatment categories where a patient can have several, crude county shares are below national benchmarks, with 95% confidence intervals excluding national values: curative 24.3% (95% CI 22.1–26.6; n=349/1437) versus 27.0% (4009/14873), life-prolonging 9.7% (95% CI 8.3–11.4; n=140/1437) versus 30.4% (4522/14873), and symptomatic 30.8% (95% CI 28.5–33.3; n=443/1437) versus 39.5% (5871/14873). `cty:curative:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:curative, cty:life_prolonging:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:life_prolonging, cty:symptomatic:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:symptomatic`
- In values digitised from Kuva 16 (about ±0.5 percentage points), the curative gap holds after age adjustment for men at 22.7% versus 26.7% nationally—with the confidence interval bar hidden inside the dot (half-width < ~3.3 percentage points) so it could not be read—but not for women at 27.4% (95% CI 23.1–31.8), as the 95% confidence interval includes 26.9%. `fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, k16:curative:men:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:men:national, k16:curative:women:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, k16:curative:women:national`
- The crude share with no treatment record within 12 months is 43.1% (95% CI 40.5–45.6; n=619/1437) versus 27.5% (4089/14873) nationally; this category mixes untreated patients and unrecorded treatment, and because its 95% confidence interval excludes and is above the national value, low crude treatment shares may partly reflect unrecorded treatment. `cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:no_treatment_record, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- A multidisciplinary team meeting is recorded for a crude 1.6% (95% CI 1.1–2.4; n=23/1437) of patients versus 6.0% (893/14873) nationally, with the 95% confidence interval excluding the national value, though this measures direct-contact recording practice in HILMO rather than multidisciplinary care itself. `cty:mdt_recorded:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, nat:mdt_recorded, bs:coverage:MDT, q:39e6ba43a9`

## How sure are we?

**Diagnosis concordance in HILMO is well supported, but county treatment tables are crude, stage is often missing, and public data omit pre-diagnostic pathways and care timestamps.** `lit:hilmo_90, fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`

- The county shares in Taulukko 11 are crude and have separate limitations: they are not age-adjusted (county-level age-adjusted shares appear only in Kuva 16), and separately they cannot be stage-adjusted because stage at diagnosis is missing for about 48% of patients. `fig:age_adjusted_only_kuva16, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f`
- A 2019 Lääkärilehti journal article shows that HILMO diagnoses match the Cancer Registry for about 90% of most cancer types, which supports diagnosis concordance only and says nothing about treatment, referral, or multidisciplinary team recording, while lung cancer data reporting, collection, and storage remain incomplete and Finland lacks a cancer quality registry. `lit:hilmo_90, q:950a2f797b, filha:registry, q:47c7e11303`
- Public report data cannot show where delays occur: symptoms and referrals are unmeasured, primary care visits are not included in the report's data, occupational health is unmentioned, imaging appears only as clinical diagnosis in about 20% of cases rather than wait times, and specialist visits (93.6% within 12 months) and treatments lack timestamps. `bs:coverage:Symptoms, bs:coverage:Primary_care_occupational_health, q:323de0d90d, bs:coverage:Imaging, q:5eb155decc, bs:coverage:Referral, bs:coverage:First_specialist_visit, bs:coverage:Treatment_start`
- Preliminary, unpublished national averages (not county-specific) indicate that lung cancer takes 155 days from symptoms to treatment in Finland versus 107 in Sweden and 67 in Denmark—with 56 days of patient delay, 41 days in primary care, and 58 days in specialist care—while the Health Care Act (§52) sets no legal maximum time for a cancer suspicion. `mot:total_fi, mot:total_se, mot:total_dk, mot:patient, mot:primary, mot:specialist, law:no_max`

## What should we do first?

**The team recommends auditing recording completeness given Varsinais-Suomi's 43.1% crude share with no treatment record within 12 months, reporting THL 21/2016 access times, and standardising referrals and coordination.** `rec:3, rec:1, rec:2, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, thl:21_2016, reg:kys_template`

- The team recommends publishing county recording completeness for stage (missing for about 48% of patients), drug treatment, and multidisciplinary coding, reading Varsinais-Suomi's overlapping crude shares—43.1% with no treatment record within 12 months, 9.7% life-prolonging treatment, and 1.6% multidisciplinary coding—as data-quality signals first, since lung cancer data reporting is incomplete and uniform recording instructions must be ensured. `rec:3, bs:coverage:Diagnosis, q:7885b0974f, cty:no_treatment_record:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:life_prolonging:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, cty:mdt_recorded:Varsinais_Suomen_hyvinvointialue, q:950a2f797b, q:82d365cd2c`
- The team recommends reporting access times from referral to first specialist visit, diagnosis, and treatment start using THL guidance Ohjaus 21/2016 definitions (covering 16 cancers) with handover alerts, since national pathway monitoring is lacking and the Filha National Lung Cancer Programme's diagnostics recommendation with recommended specialist-care time limits is to be carried out before the 2030 mid-term evaluation. `rec:1, thl:21_2016, q:24169121a4, q:4e702a20fe, q:20d6ca414e, q:432c0e51d6, q:614febebf2, q:14dff3e002`
- The team recommends a KYS-style referral template, referring persistent symptoms after a normal X-ray (featured only in KYS's pathway document), and a named diagnostic-phase contact (absent in reviewed regional pathway documents), since mentor insight notes weak referrals receive lower urgency and STM roadmap measure 1.2.3 plans care-coordinator guidance by 2026–2028 for county adoption by 2029–2031. `rec:2, reg:kys_template, reg:kys_persistent, reg:only_kys_xray, reg:named_contact, mentor:kaisa_referral, q:fc81032a80, q:abcbb06ae3, q:050ee09ebe`
